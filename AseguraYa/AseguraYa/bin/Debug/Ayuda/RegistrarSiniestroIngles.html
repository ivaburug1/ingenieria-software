🧾 Help - Register Claim

1. Purpose of this form

The Register Claim form allows users to record an incident or loss related to an active insurance policy within the AseguraYa system. The form collects the event date, property value, repair cost, and coverage information.

2. Screen structure

  • Policy Number: numeric field identifying the affected policy.
  • Search: verifies the policy and loads available coverages.
  • Date: selects the date when the incident occurred.
  • Asset Value: total insured value of the affected item.
  • Repair Cost: estimated repair or compensation value.
  • Coverages: dropdown list showing all applicable coverages.
  • Register: confirms and saves the claim to the system.

3. Registration process

  1. Enter the Policy Number and click Search.
  2. If the policy exists, available coverages will be displayed.
  3. Select the Date, fill in the asset and repair values, and choose the appropriate coverage.
  4. Click Register to save the claim.
  5. The system generates a report, recalculates verification digits (DVH/DVV), and logs the event.
💡 Tip: Always click “Search” before registering a claim. The system will block registration if the policy is not validated.

4. Validations and common errors

  • The policy number must be numeric.
  • Asset and repair values must be valid decimal numbers (e.g., 1234.50).
  • Coverage selection is mandatory before registration.
  • Empty fields are not allowed.

5. Logging and traceability

Each claim record is stored with the date, responsible user, and event details. A PDF report is automatically generated and the event is logged in the system’s audit trail.
