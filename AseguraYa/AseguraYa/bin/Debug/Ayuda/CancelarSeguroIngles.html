❌ Help - Cancel Insurance Policy

1. Purpose of this form

The Cancel Insurance form allows users to delete an active insurance policy from the AseguraYa system. This action should only be performed when a client requests policy termination or the coverage period has ended.

2. Screen structure

  • Policy Number: field where the user enters the policy number to cancel.
  • Eye button: displays the policy’s current data before deletion.
  • Reason: text field to specify the cancellation reason (mandatory).
  • Policy: grid displaying the details of the selected policy.
  • Buttons:
    • Cancel Insurance: confirms and deletes the policy from the system.
    • Cancel: closes the form without making any changes.

3. Cancellation process

  1. Enter the Policy Number and click the eye button 👁️.
  2. Verify that the displayed information is correct.
  3. Type in the Reason for cancellation.
  4. Click Cancel Insurance to confirm.
  5. The system will delete the policy, recalculate verification digits (DVH/DVV), and log the event.
⚠️ Important: Once deleted, a policy cannot be restored from the interface. Only an administrator can recover it via the Backup/Restore module.

4. Validations and common errors

  • The policy number must be numeric.
  • The reason must contain only letters.
  • A valid policy must exist to be canceled.
  • No empty fields are allowed.

5. Logging and traceability

All cancellations are automatically logged in the system’s event history, including the user who performed the action, the date, and the affected policy number.
