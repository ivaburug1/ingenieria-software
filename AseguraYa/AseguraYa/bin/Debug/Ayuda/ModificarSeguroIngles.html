🛠️ Help - Modify Insurance Policy

1. Purpose of this form

The Modify Insurance form allows users to update existing insurance policies in the AseguraYa system. It enables the user to review current coverage, change the associated plan, and extend the policy’s validity.

2. Screen structure

  • Policy Number: field to enter the policy number to be modified.
  • Eye button: loads and displays the current policy data.
  • Product: shows the type of insurance associated with the policy (life, auto, home, etc.).
  • Plans: displays the available plans and their related coverages for the selected product.
  • Buttons:
    • Accept: saves all modifications to the policy.
    • Cancel: closes the form without saving changes.

3. Modification process

  1. Enter the Policy Number and click the eye button 👁️.
  2. The system will display the current policy data, including product, plan, and coverages.
  3. Select a new Plan if you wish to modify it.
  4. Review the available coverages and confirm by clicking Accept.
  5. The policy will be updated and recorded in the system’s event log.
💡 Tip: the system automatically recalculates the verification digit (DVH/DVV) after every modification, ensuring data integrity.

4. Validations and common errors

  • The policy number must be numeric and valid.
  • The system will not modify non-existent policies.
  • The Product field cannot be left empty.

5. Logging and traceability

Every modification is automatically logged with the user, date, and updated details. This ensures full traceability and accountability for all policy changes.
