🧾 Help - Generate Contract

1. Purpose of this form

The Generate Contract form allows users to create new insurance policies in the AseguraYa system. The process validates the client’s information, selected product, available plans, and their related coverages.

2. Screen structure

  • DNI: identifies the client. If the client doesn’t exist, the system will open the “Register Client” window.
  • Product: dropdown menu with available insurance types (auto, home, life, etc.).
  • Plans: displays the plans and coverages related to the selected product.
  • Buttons:
    • Accept: generates the contract and policy.
    • Cancel: closes the form without saving changes.

3. Registration flow

  1. Enter the client’s DNI.
  2. If the client doesn’t exist, the system will open the Register Client form to create one.
  3. Select the Product and then the Plan.
  4. If any client data is missing (address, email, etc.), the system will open the Extra Data window.
  5. After confirmation, the policy will be generated and automatically printed.
💡 Tip: missing data such as email or postal code may prevent the policy from being generated. Ensure the client’s information is complete before confirming.

4. Related forms

  • Register Client: creates a new client record if the entered DNI does not exist.
  • Extra Data: allows completing missing client information required for policy generation.

5. Logging and traceability

All actions performed (client creation, policy generation, printing) are automatically recorded in the system’s event log for auditing purposes.
